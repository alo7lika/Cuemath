# Task D - November operations plan

## Weekly volumes

- Goal: 75 induction attendees each week for four weeks (300 total).
- September snapshot signup-to-induction rate: 155 / 3,377 = 4.59%. Use as a conservative rate while the cohort matures: 75 / 0.0459 = about **1,634 signups per week** (about 6,536 over four weeks).
- September interview-to-induction rate: 155 / 300 = 51.7%. Estimate 75 / 0.517 = about **145 interview slots per week**.
- These are snapshot planning rates, not guarantees. Start the funnel early enough for interview scheduling and Monday induction dates.

## Capacity

12 interviewers x 9 interviews = **108 slots per week**, about 37 below the estimate. Add five trained temporary interviewers (17 x 9 = 153 weekly slots); this closes the measured gap with a small buffer. Trade-off: temporary onboarding and calibration.

Other options: more interview blocks for current staff (fast, but conflicts with the nine-interview cap); improve earlier funnel conversion and scheduling (sustainable, but uncertain and unlikely to cover the immediate gap).
